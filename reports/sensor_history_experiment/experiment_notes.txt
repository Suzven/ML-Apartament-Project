Experiment: baseline polynomial degree 3 + Ridge versus polynomial degree 3 on the same 12 inputs + 77 linear sensor-history features + Ridge.
Raw input block: 454 polynomial features; augmented block: 531 total features.
History: 11 sensors, rolling means/std/OLS slopes over 10 and 30 cycles, and delta from causal initial mean of up to first 25 observed cycles.
Current sample is included in every rolling window. No future cycles are used.
Initial level at cycle t<=25 uses only cycles <=t; at t>25 uses first 25 cycles.
Same five deterministic GroupKFold engine splits and same 8 alpha candidates in both runs.
All scalers fitted inside CV folds. Validation did not influence alpha selection.
History preprocessing leaves targets and sample indices unchanged.
Baseline alpha=100; augmented alpha=1000.
Active launcher configuration was not changed by this experiment.
Causality verified by perturbing future values and comparing full trajectories with truncated prefixes.
Plots show smoothed predictions; reported metrics use raw predictions.
